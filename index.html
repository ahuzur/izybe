06 Looking ahead

AI roadmap · Not yet available

Another set of eyes.
On your operation.

We are working towards AI assistance for future izybe.app updates: an operational assistant designed to help oversee your workflow, much like an extra manager alongside your team.

Our team is giving particular attention to this direction — exploring how AI could identify work that needs attention, clarify priorities and help teams follow through on daily responsibilities.

The ambition is a smoother, more consistent operation with fewer overlooked tasks. AI would support people, not replace managerial judgement or guarantee error-free results.

  • Workflow oversight

    Potential tools could review operational activity, highlight incomplete work and bring possible issues to the team's attention.

  • Manager-like guidance

    The proposed assistant could help organise priorities and suggest next steps, with staff and managers remaining in control.

  • A clearer daily picture

    We are exploring operational summaries and useful prompts to help teams understand what has been done and what still needs attention.

Local AI?
We're exploring it.

One approach under consideration is local or offline AI processing, intended to reduce the need to send sensitive company information to external AI services. We want careful control over where operational data is processed.

This is a design direction, not a final architecture. Cloud AI security depends on the provider, configuration and data-handling arrangements; local processing also requires appropriate safeguards. Neither approach is automatically risk-free.

Future plans, not a current product capability. AI assistance has not been released, and no release date is confirmed. Features, timing and implementation may change as research and testing continue. We may choose a local, cloud, hybrid or different approach, or defer parts of the plan. A possible offline AI component does not mean the current cloud platform works offline.

11 MODULES / PRACTICAL USER GUIDE

01Lists & checklist templates

Create reusable Checklist or To-Do templates, start active copies and retain completed records in history.

Create and use a template

  1. Open Add Lists and choose a template type. Enter a title, category and daily, morning, evening or custom frequency. Custom schedules include a date and time.
  2. Add items with low, medium or high priorities. Include temperature, weight, quantity or volume fields where needed.
  3. Use Add Tracked Items to link stock products. Review the summary and save.
  4. Choose Start List to create an active copy. Complete items, enter measurements and finish the list.

Find and review work

Search and filter by category, date, frequency and list type. Active lists show ten results per page. Completed Lists contains archived details and completion records.

Editing or deleting a template does not change existing active lists. Stock-linked quantities are reflected in inventory when the list is completed.

02Staff management & working hours

Manage employee records, attendance, breaks and working-hours reports. Authentication can use a PIN or configured face recognition.

Add and manage staff

  1. Log in to Staff Management and press Add Staff. Enter first name, last name and a unique staff number.
  2. Choose full-time, part-time or agency status, position and department. Agency staff require an agency.
  3. Set a four-digit PIN and optionally add a photo for face recognition.
  4. Search by name or number, or filter by category, position, department and agency.

Attendance and reporting

Enter a staff number and verify identity in Time to Start Work. Start work, take a break or clock out according to current status. Active Staff List shows current workers; Clock Report provides attendance, break and net working-time records.

Editing requires admin access or editStaff permission. Deleting staff makes the record inactive while preserving working history.

03Main Stock & warehouse organization

Organize the warehouse through shelves, sub-shelves and products, with breadcrumb navigation showing the current position.

Build your warehouse

  1. Add a shelf with its name and optional color or icon. A shelf created inside another becomes a sub-shelf.
  2. Add products with name, quantity, minimum quantity and unit.
  3. Optionally add barcode, product code, category, location description and catalog image.
  4. Use Bulk Add to enter several products through a table-style interface.

Search and export

Search across shelf levels by name, barcode, product code or location. Use barcode scanning, filter by stock status and export current shelves and items to PDF.

Deleting a shelf permanently deletes its sub-shelves and products. Review the contents before confirming.

04Stock, reservations & transfers

Manage daily inventory by location. A cart groups products for transactions and temporarily reserves selected quantities.

Complete a transaction

  1. Open a location and add products with name, quantity, minimum quantity, unit and optional barcode or category.
  2. Add quantities to the cart using the plus button beside products.
  3. Choose Purchase to increase stock or Transfer to move products to another location.
  4. Enter supplier, price or destination information as required and complete the transaction.

Review movements

The statistics menu provides purchase, transfer and bulk-add history, plus low-stock alerts. Product search operates within the current location.

A warning appears after three minutes of inactivity. Reservations are released after five minutes unless activity resumes.

05Market & internal stock orders

Staff request products from Main Stock and Stock. Managers review and process those orders.

Create and process an order

  1. Select New Order, requesting staff, destination and stock source.
  2. Find products, adjust quantities and add an optional note. Both stock sources can contribute to one order.
  3. Place the order with pending status.
  4. Open order details and choose Process, Complete or Cancel.

Find orders

Search by staff, destination or product. Filter by date and pending, processing, completed or cancelled status.

Pending and processing orders remain visible regardless of the date filter. Orders are cancelled rather than directly deleted.

06Weekly shift planning

Create category-based weekly plans, organize staff into sub-groups and publish daily assignments.

Plan the week

  1. Log in to Shift Planner and create a plan with title and category.
  2. Select a Monday start date. Sunday is calculated as the end date.
  3. Add staff, daily start and end times, and shift types.
  4. Publish the plan. Update assignments in edit mode and publish changes.

View schedules

Search plans by name, category or date. Details show a weekly calendar. Staff Shift Plan lets employees view their schedule using their staff number.

The shift plan list requires staff login. Deleting a plan is permanent.

07Messaging, broadcasts & polls

Supabase Realtime supports direct conversations, polls, broadcasts, unread counts and typing indicators.

Communicate with the team

  1. Press New Conversation, find a company user and open the chat.
  2. Write a message and press Send or Enter. Conversations are ordered by the latest message.
  3. Create a poll with a question and at least two options. Multiple-choice voting is optional.
  4. Use broadcasts for announcements and edit your name, avatar and color through the profile control.

Poll results

View vote counts, percentages and the voters who selected each option. Unread counts update as messages arrive.

Messaging requires an internet connection and is disabled when offline. Users can delete only their own messages.

08Allergen & ingredient information

Maintain food and drink records with allergens, ingredients, categories and departments.

Maintain food records

  1. Enable edit mode and log in with staff credentials.
  2. Add a food name, category, department and optional image.
  3. Select contained allergens, enter ingredients and save.
  4. Open cards for details or use edit mode to update and delete records.

Filter and export

Search by name, filter by category or department and exclude specified allergens or ingredients. Export the current filtered list as PDF.

The business must verify that records are accurate and current. Filtering recorded information does not guarantee that a dish is safe for an allergy or free from cross-contact.

09Damage tracking & reports

Track stock products for damage, record quantities and review time-based and product-based statistics.

Track damage

  1. Press Add to Tracking and complete staff login.
  2. Select the stock product and damage type.
  3. Open the tracked product, select Add Damage, enter the quantity and save.
  4. Filter by product, damage type and time range. Choose grid or list view.

Analyze and export

Review total damage, today's damage, needed quantities and report counts. Export reporting as Excel, PDF or TXT.

Adding tracked products and editing require staff login. Removing a product from tracking preserves its damage history.

10Calendar tasks & history

Unlike reusable templates, these task lists belong to specific dates and can be assigned to departments and multiple staff members.

Plan and complete tasks

  1. Select a day in Todo Calendar and create a task list.
  2. Enter a title, optional department and staff assignments, then add items.
  3. Save to the chosen date. Task days are marked on the calendar.
  4. Complete items in list details. Finished lists move to history.

Filter and review

Combine department and staff filters. Inspect completed lists through History and edit existing lists through their card controls.

Completing a list preserves it in history. Deleting it is a separate, permanent action.

11Administration, settings & backups

Separate everyday preferences from administrative configuration. Admin Panel access requires administrative credentials.

User preferences

  • Update profile and appearance.
  • Configure checklist schedules and daily reset behavior.
  • Adjust sounds, message notifications and stock alerts.
  • Search activity logs and filter by activity type.

Administrative controls

  1. Configure dashboard categories, colors, visibility and damage settings.
  2. Manage accounts, roles, module access and editing permissions.
  3. Maintain departments, positions and agencies.
  4. Set PIN protection, admin credentials, PIN or face authentication and optional liveness detection.

Backup and restore

Export data as JSON, import a previous backup and configure available automatic backup settings. Review previous records in backup history.

Restoring overwrites current data and cannot be undone. With Settings PIN protection enabled, leaving that screen locks it again.

09 Frequently asked

A little clarity.
Before you begin.

Learn about cloud access, the launch and the difference between today's platform and future AI plans.

What is izybe.app?

izybe.app is a cloud-based SaaS platform for restaurant and hotel operations. It brings staff, checklists, stock, shifts, internal orders, messaging and operational records into one browser-based workspace.

Does it work on iOS, Android, Windows, Linux and macOS?

Yes, through a compatible modern browser and an internet connection. Supported phones, tablets and computers can access the platform. Camera features depend on device capabilities and permissions. This is browser access, not a claim of separate native apps for every system.

Is the system cloud-based and real-time?

Yes. The platform supports real-time operational workflows while connected. An internet connection is required for cloud access and real-time updates.

How long has the application been in development?

izybe.app has been in development for two years, bringing hospitality workflows into a connected system covering staff, tasks, stock and reporting.

Is it already being used in London?

The application is currently being used and tested in demo form at selected large restaurants in London. These are demo deployments and trials ahead of the commercial launch.

When will it be available for purchase?

Commercial availability is coming soon. A launch date and pricing have not yet been announced here. Contact info@izybe.app with product or launch enquiries.

Does izybe.app have AI assistance today?

No. AI assistance is a future roadmap initiative, not an available feature. The team is actively exploring its design and possible integration for later updates. No release date is confirmed.

What could the planned AI assistant do?

The goal is manager-like support for daily workflows: helping highlight incomplete work, clarify priorities, summarise activity and suggest next steps. These are proposed capabilities, not final specifications. Staff and managers would remain responsible for decisions.

Why are you considering local or offline AI?

We want to minimise unnecessary transfer of sensitive company information to external AI services and retain control over data processing. Local or offline inference is one option under consideration. It is not yet implemented or selected as the final approach, and local processing would still need appropriate security controls.

Could the AI integration plan change?

Yes. Research, testing, hardware requirements and operational needs may lead to local, cloud, hybrid or another implementation. Features and timing may change, and parts of the plan may be deferred. Roadmap descriptions are not a commitment to a specific release.

Would offline AI make the whole application work offline?

Not necessarily. A local AI component and an offline application are different capabilities. The current cloud platform requires internet access; exploring local AI does not change that requirement.

Will AI make every workflow flawless?

No software or AI can guarantee perfect outcomes. Our aim is to reduce overlooked tasks and improve consistency and visibility. Any future AI suggestions would still require appropriate human review.

What is the difference between Main Stock and Stock?

Main Stock organizes the warehouse through nested shelves. Stock manages daily inventory by location, including reservations, purchases and transfers. Market orders can use products from both sources.

Which reports can be exported?

Main Stock and Allergens provide PDF exports. Damage Statistics supports Excel, PDF and TXT. Administrative backup and restore uses JSON.

Where can I follow launch updates?

Check this website and the Contact section. Official Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn links are coming soon. You can also email us with questions.

Are the screens on this website live?

No. This is a product introduction and user guide. The application screens are screenshots rather than live staff, inventory or messaging interfaces.
