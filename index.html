01

Lists & checklist templates

Create reusable Checklist or To-Do templates, start active lists from them and retain completed records in history.

Create and use a template

  1. Open Add Lists and choose a Checklist or To-Do template. Enter a title, category and frequency: daily, morning, evening or custom. Custom schedules include a date and time.
  2. Add items with low, medium or high priorities. Include temperature, weight, quantity or volume fields where needed.
  3. Use Add Tracked Items to link stock products. Review the summary and save the template.
  4. Choose Start List to create an active copy. Complete items, enter measurements and finish the list.

Find and review work

Search and filter by category, date, frequency and list type. Active lists are paginated with ten per page. Completed Lists provides archived details and completion records.

Editing or deleting a template does not change existing active lists. Quantities entered in stock-linked items are reflected in stock when the list is completed.
02

Staff management & working hours

Manage employee records, clock-in and clock-out events, breaks, active staff and working-hours reports. Authentication can use a PIN or configured face recognition.

Add and manage staff

  1. Log in to Staff Management and press Add Staff. Enter first name, last name and a unique staff number.
  2. Choose full-time, part-time or agency status, then select position and department. Agency staff require an agency.
  3. Set a four-digit PIN and optionally add a photo for face recognition.
  4. Search by name or number, or filter by category, position, department and agency.

Attendance and reporting

In Time to Start Work, enter a staff number and verify identity. Start work, manage a break or clock out according to current status. Active Staff List shows current workers. Clock Report provides date-filtered attendance, break and net working-time records.

Editing requires admin access or editStaff permission. Deleting staff makes the record inactive; historical working records are preserved.
03

Main Stock & warehouse organization

Organize the warehouse through shelves, sub-shelves and products. Breadcrumb navigation identifies the current location in the hierarchy.

Build your warehouse

  1. Add a shelf with its name and optional color or icon. Shelves created inside another shelf become sub-shelves.
  2. Open a shelf and add products with name, quantity, minimum quantity and unit.
  3. Optionally add barcode, product code, category, location description and catalog image.
  4. Use Bulk Add to enter several products through a table-style interface.

Search and export

Search across shelf levels using product names, barcodes, product codes or locations. Barcode scanning can start a search. Filter sufficient, low and out-of-stock products and export the current shelves and items to PDF.

Deleting a shelf permanently deletes its sub-shelves and products. Review the contents before confirming.
04

Operational stock, reservations & transfers

Manage daily inventory by location. A cart groups products for transactions and temporarily reserves selected quantities.

Complete a transaction

  1. Open a location and add products with name, quantity, minimum quantity, unit and optional barcode or category.
  2. Use the plus button beside products to add quantities to the cart and reserve them.
  3. Choose Purchase to increase stock or Transfer to move products to another location.
  4. Enter supplier, price or destination information as required and complete the transaction.

Review movements

The statistics menu provides purchase history, transfer history, low-stock alerts and bulk-add history. Product search operates within the current location.

A warning appears after three minutes of inactivity. Reservations are automatically released after five minutes unless activity resumes.
05

Market & internal stock orders

Staff request products from Main Stock and Stock. Managers review orders and move them through the ordering workflow.

Create and process an order

  1. Select New Order, requesting staff, destination and stock source.
  2. Find products, adjust quantities and add an optional note. Both stock sources can contribute to one order.
  3. Place the order with pending status.
  4. Open order details and choose Process, Complete or Cancel.

Find orders

Search by staff, destination or product, and filter by date and pending, processing, completed or cancelled status.

Pending and processing orders remain visible regardless of the date filter. Orders are cancelled rather than directly deleted.
06

Weekly shift planning

Create category-based weekly plans, organize staff into sub-groups and publish daily assignments.

Plan the week

  1. Log in to Shift Planner and create a plan with a title and category.
  2. Select a Monday start date. The Sunday end date is calculated automatically.
  3. Add staff, daily start and end times, and shift types.
  4. Publish the plan. Update assignments in edit mode and publish changes.

View schedules

Search published plans by name, category or date. Details show a Monday-to-Sunday calendar. Staff Shift Plan lets employees view their own schedule using their staff number.

The shift plan list requires staff login. Deleting a plan is permanent.
07

Team messaging, broadcasts & polls

Supabase Realtime supports direct conversations, polls, broadcasts, unread counts and typing indicators.

Communicate with the team

  1. Press New Conversation, find a company user and open the chat.
  2. Write a message and press Send or Enter. Conversations are ordered by the latest message.
  3. Create a poll with a question and at least two options. Multiple-choice voting is optional.
  4. Use broadcasts for announcements and edit your display name, avatar and color through the profile control.

Poll results

View vote counts, percentages and the voters who selected each option. Unread counts update as messages arrive.

Messaging requires an internet connection and is disabled when offline. Users can delete only their own messages.
08

Allergen & ingredient information

Maintain food and drink records with allergens, ingredients, categories and departments.

Maintain food records

  1. Enable edit mode and log in with staff credentials.
  2. Add a food name, category, department and optional image.
  3. Select contained allergens, enter ingredients and save.
  4. Open cards for details or use edit mode to update and delete records.

Filter and export

Search by name, filter by category or department, and exclude specified allergens or ingredients. Export the current filtered list as PDF.

The business must check that records are accurate and current. Filtering recorded information does not guarantee that a dish is safe for a particular allergy or free from cross-contact.
09

Damage tracking & operational reports

Track stock products for damage, record quantities and review time-based and product-based statistics.

Track damage

  1. Press Add to Tracking in Damage Reports and complete staff login.
  2. Select the stock product and damage type.
  3. Open the tracked product, select Add Damage, enter the quantity and save.
  4. Filter by product, damage type and time range. Choose grid or list view.

Analyze and export

Review total damage, today's damage, needed quantities and today's report count. Export trends and product-based reporting as Excel, PDF or TXT.

Adding tracked products and editing require staff login. Removing a product from tracking preserves its damage history.
10

Calendar tasks & completion history

Unlike reusable templates, these task lists belong to specific dates and can be assigned to departments and multiple staff members.

Plan and complete tasks

  1. Select a day in Todo Calendar and create a new task list.
  2. Enter a title, optional department and staff assignments, then add task items.
  3. Save to the chosen date. Task days are marked on the monthly calendar.
  4. Complete items in list details. Finished lists move to history.

Filter and review

Combine department and staff filters. Inspect completed lists through History and edit existing lists through their card controls.

Completing a list preserves it in history. Deleting a list is a separate, permanent action.
11

Settings, administration & backups

Separate everyday preferences from administrative configuration. Admin Panel access requires administrative credentials.

User preferences

  • Update profile and appearance.
  • Configure checklist schedules and daily reset behavior.
  • Adjust sounds, message notifications and low-stock alerts.
  • Search activity logs and filter by activity type.

Administrative controls

  1. Configure dashboard categories, colors, visibility and damage settings.
  2. Manage accounts, roles, module access and editing permissions.
  3. Maintain departments, positions and agencies.
  4. Set PIN protection, admin credentials, PIN or face authentication and optional liveness detection.

Backup and restore

Export application data as JSON, import a previous backup and configure available automatic backup settings. Review previous records in backup history.

Restoring overwrites current data and cannot be undone. With Settings PIN protection enabled, leaving that screen locks it again.

08 / Frequently asked questions

A little clarity.
Before you begin.

Learn about the cloud platform, device access, current demo stage and the workflows available to hospitality teams.

What is izybe.app?

izybe.app is a cloud-based Software as a Service platform for restaurant and hotel operations. It brings staff, checklists, stock, shifts, internal orders, messaging and operational records together in a browser-based workspace.

Does it work on iOS, Android, Windows, Linux and macOS?

Yes, through a compatible modern web browser and an internet connection. You can access the platform from supported phones, tablets and computers. Camera features depend on device capabilities and browser permissions; this is browser access, not a claim of separate native apps for every operating system.

Is the platform cloud-based and real-time?

Yes. izybe.app is a cloud-based SaaS system with real-time operational workflows. Connected users can work with updated operational information across supported devices. An internet connection is needed for cloud access and real-time updates.

Who is it designed for?

The platform is designed for restaurant and hotel operators who need to coordinate staff, daily checks, departmental tasks, inventory, internal requests and operational reporting. The focus is the teams and processes behind day-to-day hospitality operations.

How long has izybe.app been in development?

izybe.app has been in development for two years. Work has focused on bringing hospitality workflows into a connected system, from staff and lists to stock, shifts and reporting.

Is it already being used in London?

The application is currently being used and tested in demo form at selected large restaurants in London. These are demo deployments and trials ahead of the commercial launch.

When will it be available for purchase?

Commercial availability is coming soon. A launch date and pricing have not yet been announced on this website. Check back for updates or contact izybe.app@gmail.com with launch enquiries.

What is the difference between Main Stock and Stock?

Main Stock organizes a warehouse through nested shelves and products. Stock manages daily inventory by location, with reservations, purchases and transfers. Market orders can include products from both sources.

How are list templates different from calendar tasks?

Lists creates reusable templates that produce new active checklists or task lists. Calendar tasks belong to specific dates and can be assigned to departments and staff. Both preserve completion records.

Can checklist items be linked to stock?

Yes. Add Tracked Items lets you select stock products when preparing a template. These receive quantity fields, and entered quantities are reflected in stock when the list is completed.

Which reports can be exported?

Main Stock and Allergens provide PDF exports. Damage Statistics supports Excel, PDF and TXT. Administrative backup and restore uses JSON.

Where can I follow launch updates?

Check this website and the Contact section. Our official Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn links are coming soon. You can also email us directly with product or launch questions.

Are the screens on this website live?

No. This is a product introduction and user guide. The displayed application screens are screenshots, not live staff, inventory or messaging interfaces.
